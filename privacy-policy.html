Privacy Policy

Phynix Tech Services ("Phynix Tech", "we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to upholding rigorous data privacy and confidentiality standards for our enterprise partners, site visitors, and clients.

Last Updated: March 30, 2026

1. Scope & Overview

This Privacy Policy outlines how Phynix Tech Services handles information when you visit our official web properties or interact with our enterprise team. Headquartered in Dallas, Texas, our practices align with applicable data protection regulations and enterprise governance standards.

2. Information We Collect

We adhere to data minimization principles. Our web architecture does not run tracking cookies, behavioral ad pixels, or invasive third-party telemetry.

  • Direct Communication Data: Information you voluntarily provide when contacting us via email (e.g., name, professional email address, company name, project requirements).
  • Technical Web Server Logs: Standard web request parameters logged for operational security and network health (e.g., IP address, access timestamp, user agent).
  • Local Storage Preference: Your selected visual theme preference (light or dark) stored strictly in browser localStorage.

3. How We Use Information

Collected information is strictly processed for legitimate corporate and engineering communication purposes:

  • Responding to enterprise service inquiries, project scoping requests, and technical consultations.
  • Fulfilling contractual obligations for active client engineering projects under formal Master Services Agreements (MSAs).
  • Maintaining web security, protecting network integrity, and preventing abuse or malicious cyber activity.

4. Data Sharing & Third Parties

Phynix Tech Services does not sell, rent, license, or trade client or user data under any circumstances. We do not share personal data with external marketers. Information may only be disclosed if required by law or binding legal process.

5. Data Security & Storage

We employ enterprise-grade administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to prevent unauthorized data access, disclosure, or alteration. All client communications are subject to non-disclosure agreements and zero-trust internal access controls.

6. Privacy Contacts & Rights
